How To Read This

Driver tags are the short labels attached to each settled Live Lens BET. They explain why the signal fired, like pace running fast, efficiency running cold, or a half-market guardrail making ATS thresholds stricter.

The guide below follows the NHL Live Lens accuracy page pattern: plain-English definitions for the tags you see in the totals and ATS recap tables, plus a smaller family guide that groups similar tags together.
